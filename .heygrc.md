# heyGRC compliance context

Self-described context about this (fictional) company so heyGRC's pull-request reviews can reference its own systems, data and controls. This is context for review, not a compliance attestation.

## Frameworks in scope for review
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- SOC 2
- GDPR
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023
- EU AI Act

## Self-described company context
- Sector: `B2B SaaS (sales workflow)`
- Data types handled: `user accounts, CRM contacts of customers, call notes`
- Hosting region: `EU only`

## Systems and controls to emphasize
- AI systems are listed in `docs/compliance/ai-system-inventory.md`. A model, provider, region or retention change must update it and re-run `docs/compliance/ai-impact-assessment.md` before merge.
- The CRM sync agent (AI-02) must get per-change human approval before any CRM write (AI impact assessment, section 4).
- Every vendor that receives personal data is listed in `docs/compliance/records-of-processing.md` with a DPA and, outside the EU, a transfer safeguard.
- Every AI surface shows the notice from `src/web/aiNotice.ts` and is listed in `docs/compliance/ai-transparency-inventory.md` (EU AI Act Art. 50(1)).
